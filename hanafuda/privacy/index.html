Hanafuda Sensei Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026

Hanafuda Sensei is a hanafuda (Koi-Koi) game from okamotodesigns, for Android and iPhone.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking, and it doesn't use the internet. Nothing about you or your games is sent to okamotodesigns or anyone else.

What stays on your device

The match in progress, so you can pick up where you left off, and whether you've seen the welcome screen. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, removes both.

Links

The About screen links to the card art's source and license. Tapping one opens your browser, which handles that visit under its own privacy policy.

What the app stores may collect

Google Play, the Apple App Store, and TestFlight handle downloads and may collect information under their own privacy policies. okamotodesigns may see the summary install, crash, and tester reports those stores provide to developers.

Children

okamotodesigns collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
