Hanafuda Sensei

Learn and play Koi-Koi, from okamotodesigns

For my dad, Kazuto, and my uncle, Matsuo, who patiently taught me to play when I was a child.

This is an early test version for family. It's free, with no ads and no account. Pick your phone:

iPhone

  1. Get the free TestFlight app from the App Store.
  2. Come back here and tap: Join on iPhone
  3. TestFlight opens. Tap Accept, then Install.

Android

  1. Tap Join the tester group, then Join group. Use the same Google account as your Play Store.
  2. Then tap Get it on Google Play, tap Become a tester, then Download it on Google Play. Google Play may call it com.okamotodesigns.hanafuda for now. That's the right one.

Something broken or confusing? Tell me. That's the whole point of a beta.
