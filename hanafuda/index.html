Hanafuda Sensei

Learn and play Koi-Koi and Sakura, from okamotodesigns

For my dad, Kazuto, and my uncle, Matsuo, who patiently taught me to play when I was a child.

Beta testers wanted. Hanafuda is the Japanese flower card game. Play Koi-Koi, its classic two-player game, or Sakura, the Hawaii-style game where every card you capture counts. Hanafuda Sensei teaches you as you play, with a coach and pictured rules. It's free, with no ads and no account. Try it, and tell me where it lost you. Pick your phone:

iPhone

  1. Get the free TestFlight app from the App Store.
  2. Come back here and tap: Join on iPhone
  3. TestFlight opens. Tap Accept, then Install.

Android

  1. Tap Get it on Google Play, then Install. It's listed as early access while it's in beta.

Something broken or confusing? Tell me. That's the whole point of a beta.
