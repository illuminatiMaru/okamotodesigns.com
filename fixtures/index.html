Photon Fixtures

A fixture and patch app from okamotodesigns, in private testing

The answers you go hunting for in a PDF manual at load-in, on your phone: what a fixture's modes are, what every channel does, how many fit in a universe, and where each one lands in the patch.

What it does

  • Search hundreds of fixtures and pick a mode to see every DMX channel, its default, and its value ranges.
  • Chain math: how many fit in a universe with no fixture split, and the total channels for any count.
  • Build a patch: add fixtures by quantity and position, start new universes, and get each unit's address and fixture number.
  • Export a clean patch sheet as a PDF or spreadsheet to print or send to the crew.
  • Works offline. No ads, no accounts needed for the built-in library.

Patch sheet export

Fixture definitions are community-written. Check them against the manufacturer's chart before you trust them on a show.

Want to test it?

Photon Fixtures is in private testing on Android. If you program or patch rigs and want to try it, send me a note.
