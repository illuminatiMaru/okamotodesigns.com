Photon Fixtures Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

Photon Fixtures is a lighting fixture library and patch app from okamotodesigns, for Android and iPhone.

The short version

okamotodesigns doesn't collect your data. The app has no ads, no analytics, and no okamotodesigns account. What you build in it stays on your device.

What stays on your device

  • Your shows, patches, custom fixtures, favorites, and settings.
  • Fixture files and the fixture list you download from GDTF Share.
  • Whether you've bought the unlock, so it keeps working with no signal.

Deleting your data

Everything the app keeps is on your device, so you can delete it yourself at any time:

  • Everything: uninstall the app, or clear its storage (Android: Settings > Apps > Photon Fixtures > Storage > Clear storage). This removes your shows, patches, custom fixtures, favorites, settings, downloaded fixture files, and the saved GDTF Share sign-in.
  • GDTF Share sign-in only: sign out on the About screen. This deletes the saved password and the downloaded GDTF files.
  • A single show: delete it from the show menu on the Patch screen.

okamotodesigns holds no copy of any of it, so there is nothing to request from us. Your GDTF Share account itself is managed at gdtf-share.com, and purchase records are kept by Google Play or the Apple App Store. Questions: shadow@okamotodesigns.com.

GDTF Share sign-in (optional)

If you sign in to GDTF Share, the app sends your username and password only to gdtf-share.com, over an encrypted connection, to get the fixture list and fixture files. Your password is stored encrypted on your device (the Android Keystore, or the iPhone Keychain) so the app can sign in again for you. It is never sent to okamotodesigns. Signing out deletes the saved password and the downloaded GDTF files. GDTF Share handles your account under its own privacy policy.

Library sync (optional)

The Sync button on the About screen downloads Open Fixture Library updates from GitHub. The request carries nothing about you beyond what any web request does, such as your IP address, which GitHub handles under its own privacy policy.

Exports

Export creates a PDF and a CSV on your device. They only leave it when you choose where to send them.

Purchases

The optional unlock is sold through Google Play or the Apple App Store, which handle payment. okamotodesigns never sees your card details. The app only learns whether you own the unlock. okamotodesigns may see the order records the stores provide to developers.

What the app stores may collect

Google Play and the Apple App Store handle downloads and may collect information under their own privacy policies. okamotodesigns may see the summary sales, install, and crash reports those stores provide to developers.

Children

The app isn't directed at children, and okamotodesigns collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
