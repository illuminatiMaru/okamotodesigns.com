Photon BeamCalc Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

Photon BeamCalc is a lighting beam calculator from okamotodesigns, for Android and iPhone.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, ads, or analytics, and it doesn't use the internet. The numbers you enter stay on your device and are never sent to okamotodesigns or anyone else.

What stays on your device

The app remembers whether you chose feet or meters. Uninstalling the app removes that setting.

What the app stores may collect

Google Play and the Apple App Store handle downloads and may collect information under their own privacy policies. okamotodesigns may see the summary sales, install, and crash reports those stores provide to developers.

Children

The app isn't directed at children and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
