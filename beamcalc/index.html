Photon BeamCalc

A lighting beam calculator from okamotodesigns

Photon BeamCalc answers the questions you get on a site visit or in the middle of a focus call, without a laptop or a signal.

What it does

  • Throw distance and the angle off straight down, from floor distance and trim height, to the floor or to a face.
  • Beam size from lens angle and throw, lens angle from throw and beam size, or the throw you need for a beam size.
  • Raked floor pools: the pool's length and width, and where its near and far edges land.
  • Feet and inches or meters. Works offline. No accounts, ads, or data collection.

Results assume a round beam on a level floor. Check them before you rely on them on a show. Photon BeamCalc calculates lighting beams, not structural beams, and it isn't for rigging or load calculations.

Android on Google Play soon. iPhone in development. Privacy policy.
